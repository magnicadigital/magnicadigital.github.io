Hoops Timer Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Hoops Timer ("the app") is a basketball countdown timer developed and published by magnicadigital. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Data collection

Hoops Timer collects no personal information. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, including:

  • No name, email, phone number, or location
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage analytics or crash reports
  • No account, login, or registration

Permissions

The app requires no special permissions. It only keeps the screen on while a game is running, which is a standard display behavior and does not access any personal data.

Local data

The only data the app stores is your chosen timer length (e.g. 10 minutes), saved locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising, and no analytics services. It does not require network access to function.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
